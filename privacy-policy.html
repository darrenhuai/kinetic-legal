Kinetic Analyzer

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 8, 2026

Kinetic Analyzer ("the App", "we", "us"), operated by Darren Huai, provides technique feedback for combat-sports training. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices.

Information we collect

  • Account information — your email address and name, used to create and secure your account.
  • Content you provide — the video clips you submit for analysis and the body-motion landmarks and technique metrics derived from them.
  • Basic technical data — information needed to operate the service (e.g. request logs). We do not use third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.

How we use your information

  • To provide the analysis and coaching feedback you request.
  • To create and maintain your account.
  • To operate, secure, and improve the App.

How your video is handled

Clips are sent to our pose-analysis service to compute body-motion landmarks. Clips submitted for one-off analysis are processed and then deleted from the analysis service. Clips and results you choose to save are stored in your account until you delete them or your account.

Who we share it with

We use service providers to run the App and do not sell your personal data:

  • Supabase — authentication, database, and storage.
  • Our analysis service host — transient video processing.

These providers process data only to provide their service to us.

Data retention and deletion

You can permanently delete your account at any time in Settings → Delete Account. This removes your account and associated sessions and results. You may also contact us at kineticanalyzer.app@gmail.com for help.

Security

We use industry-standard measures (encryption in transit, access controls) to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g. under GDPR or CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data. Use in-app deletion or contact kineticanalyzer.app@gmail.com.

Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions? Contact Darren Huai at kineticanalyzer.app@gmail.com.
